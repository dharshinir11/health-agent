CityCare General Hospital - Emergency Care & Urgent Medical Guidelines

CRITICAL EMERGENCY WARNING:
If you or someone around you is experiencing life-threatening medical symptoms, DO NOT book an appointment online or wait for a scheduled consultation. IMMEDIATELY call emergency services (911 / 112 / +1 (800) 555-9111) or proceed directly to the nearest hospital Emergency Room (ER).

Red Flag Emergency Symptoms:
1. Cardiovascular:
   - Severe chest pain, pressure, fullness, or squeezing sensation in the center of the chest.
   - Pain radiating to the jaw, neck, back, stomach, or one or both arms.
   - Cold sweats accompanied by chest tightness and sudden dizziness.
2. Respiratory:
   - Severe shortness of breath, sudden inability to catch breath, or gasping.
   - Blue or pale tint around the lips, tongue, or nail beds (cyanosis).
3. Neurological / Stroke (FAST Protocol):
   - Face drooping: One side of the face droops or is numb when smiling.
   - Arm weakness: One arm feels weak or numb when raising both arms.
   - Speech difficulty: Slurred speech or inability to speak coherently.
   - Sudden severe "thunderclap" headache, loss of vision, or confusion.
4. Other Emergencies:
   - Active, uncontrolled, heavy bleeding that cannot be stopped with direct pressure.
   - Loss of consciousness, fainting, or unresponsiveness.
   - Seizures or convulsions lasting more than 2 minutes or recurring seizures.
   - Severe allergic reaction (anaphylaxis) with swelling of throat, lips, tongue, or wheezing.
   - Suicidal thoughts, severe self-harm ideation, or acute psychiatric distress.
