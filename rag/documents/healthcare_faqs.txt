CityCare General Hospital - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: Does CityCare General Hospital accept health insurance plans?
A1: Yes, CityCare General Hospital partners with over 45 major health insurance providers, government healthcare schemes, and corporate health networks. Cashless hospitalization is supported for admitted patients with pre-authorization. For OPD consultations, patients may pay directly and submit bills for reimbursement.

Q2: How can I access my diagnostic lab results and radiology reports?
A2: Diagnostic lab reports are available online through the CityCare Patient Portal (https://portal.citycarehospital.org) within 6-12 hours for routine blood tests, and 24 hours for radiology imaging (CT/MRI/X-Ray). An SMS notification with a secure download link is sent to your registered mobile number once reports are signed off.

Q3: Can I book a teleconsultation or video appointment?
A3: Yes, teleconsultations are available for follow-up visits, routine reviews, and general consultations across General Medicine, Dermatology, and Pediatrics. In-person physical visits are mandatory for initial orthopedic assessments, interventional cardiology, or procedure-related treatments.

Q4: Are wheelchair services and special assistance available for elderly patients?
A4: Yes, complimentary wheelchair services and dedicated patient assistance attendants are stationed at the main hospital entrance, emergency ramp, and parking lobbies. Escorts can accompany elderly and differently-abled patients throughout their clinic visits.

Q5: What is the hospital visitor policy for admitted inpatients?
A5: In general wards, one primary attendant pass is issued per patient for 24-hour bedside presence. Visiting hours for other family members are strictly 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 4:30 PM to 7:00 PM. In the Intensive Care Unit (ICU), visiting is restricted to 15-minute intervals between 11:00 AM and 12:00 PM, and 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM with sanitized protective attire.

Q6: What payment methods are accepted at the hospital?
A6: The hospital billing counter accepts cash, credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), UPI, mobile wallets, and direct bank wire transfers for inpatient deposits.

Q7: Where is the hospital located and what parking is available?
A7: We are located at 100 Health Avenue, Metro Medical District, City Center - 600001. A multi-level covered parking facility is available on campus with free parking for the first 2 hours for OPD visitors and round-the-clock complimentary valet service.
