CityCare General Hospital - Hospital Information & Timings

Overview:
CityCare General Hospital is a premier multi-specialty healthcare facility offering comprehensive medical services, advanced diagnostic capabilities, and compassionate patient care.

Operating Hours & Timings:
- Outpatient Department (OPD): Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Sunday OPD is open for urgent non-emergency visits from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
- Inpatient & Visiting Hours: General visiting hours are 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 4:30 PM to 7:00 PM daily. Only one visitor is permitted at the bedside at any given time.
- Emergency Department (ER): Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.
- Pharmacy Services: The central hospital pharmacy is operational 24/7. Outpatient branch pharmacy operates 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- Diagnostic Laboratory & Radiology: Routine lab sample collection runs Monday to Saturday from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM. Emergency pathology and radiology services operate 24/7.

Key Facilities & Infrastructure:
- 350 inpatient beds including specialized ICU, CCU, and NICU units.
- State-of-the-art diagnostic imaging: 3T MRI, 128-slice CT Scanner, Digital X-ray, and 4D Ultrasound.
- Automated clinical laboratory with same-day digital report delivery.
- Fully equipped minor and major surgical operation theaters.
- Wheelchair accessibility, valet parking, and dedicated ambulance fleet.
- Multi-cuisine patient cafeteria and dietary consultation services.
