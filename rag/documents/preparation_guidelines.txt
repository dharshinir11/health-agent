CityCare General Hospital - Appointment Preparation Guidelines

What to Bring for Your Appointment:
1. Government-issued Photo Identification: Driver's license, passport, or national identity card.
2. Insurance Card & Documents: Active health insurance card, pre-authorization forms, or employer coverage letter if applicable.
3. Medical History & Past Records: Previous prescription slips, discharge summaries, surgical notes, biopsy reports, or recent hospital discharge papers.
4. Recent Diagnostic Reports: Blood test results, X-rays, MRI/CT scans, and ultrasound reports taken within the last 6 months.
5. Current Medications List: A complete list (or actual containers) of all current prescription drugs, over-the-counter medications, vitamins, and dietary supplements with dosages.
6. Symptom Journal or Questions: A written log of symptoms (when they started, severity, triggers) and a list of specific questions for the physician.

Preparation Rules by Consultation Type:
- Fasting for Blood Tests / Metabolic Panels:
  * For lipid profile, fasting blood glucose, or metabolic panels, 8 to 12 hours of overnight fasting is mandatory.
  * Water is permitted, but avoid tea, coffee, sodas, and juices during the fasting window.
- Ultrasound Scans (Abdominal / Pelvic):
  * Upper abdominal ultrasound requires 6 hours of fasting.
  * Pelvic or urinary bladder ultrasound requires a full bladder; drink 4-5 glasses of water 1 hour prior to the scan and do not void.
- Dermatology Appointments:
  * Avoid applying makeup, heavy lotions, or concealers over skin lesions or facial areas to be examined.
  * Wear comfortable, loose clothing to facilitate full skin examination.
- Orthopedic Consultations:
  * Wear loose two-piece clothing or shorts to allow easy physical examination of joints, knees, and back.

Arrival Instructions:
- Arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled consultation slot to complete desk check-in and vital signs verification.
